01Log in and browse supplies

Open AMS Supplies, log in with the office account, search, filter by category and open an item.

02Request supplies (cart and RIS)

Add items to the cart, set quantities, purpose, requested by and received by, check the live RIS preview, then submit.

03Track and print the RIS

Follow the requisition in My requests and print the approved RIS on A4 for signing.

04Issued: signed RIS

After AMS releases the items, view or download the signed RIS, or request the same items again.

05My APP-CSE

The office's yearly plan per item: per quarter, issued, pending and remaining.

06Suggest a supply

Ask AMS to carry an item that is not in the catalog, then follow the answer.

07Announcements

Notices from AMS shown at the top of every page.

08Help, What's new, dark mode, password

Help and FAQ, the list of updates, dark mode and changing the office password.

09On a phone

The catalog, My requests and My APP-CSE on a phone screen.

01Sign in and dashboard

Sign in to AMS StockWatch; the dashboard shows pending requisitions, stock levels and property.

02Review and approve a requisition

Check stock and APP-CSE left, adjust quantities with a remark, choose signatories and approve.

03Reject a requisition

Reject with a reason that the office sees; nothing is deducted.

04Release items (mark as issued)

Choose who received and issued the items, attach the signed RIS (PDF) and mark it as issued.

05Answer supply suggestions

Set the status of an office's suggestion and reply to it.

06Catalog: add an item with a photo

Add a new supply with price, low-stock level, category and supplier, then add its photo.

07Inventory and stock card

Receive a delivery, adjust stock after a count, open the stock card and print it (Appendix 58).

08APP-CSE

View each office's allocation, download the Excel template and upload it with a check before importing.

09Reports and RSMI

Monthly RSMI, Generate RSMI and print it, APP-CSE utilization and the reorder list.

10SPLV and SPHV deliveries

Record a semi-expendable delivery; ICS/PAR numbers are assigned automatically.

11Property register

Filter property by status and add a new property with its accountable person.

12Property documents

Upload a PAR/ICS with the date signed, and use the Returned switch to upload a Return Slip.

13Employees

Add Regional Office personnel so they can be chosen on RIS and property documents.

14Announcements

Post a notice that every office sees in AMS Supplies.

15Accounts and activity log

Create an office account, reset a password and review the activity log.

16Settings, profile and dark mode

RIS settings, office RIS codes and shared APP-CSE, profile picture and dark mode.
